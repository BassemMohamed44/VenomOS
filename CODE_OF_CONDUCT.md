# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the VenomOS
community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful
- Accepting constructive criticism
- Helping new contributors
- Focusing on improving the project

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment
- Personal attacks
- Trolling
- Discrimination

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for enforcing this Code of Conduct and may
remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate these rules.

Thank you for helping make VenomOS a welcoming project.
